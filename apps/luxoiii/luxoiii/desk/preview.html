Camera calibration & assumptions

The camera moves with the head pivot. Starting assumptions: 60° horizontal view, 16 cm face width, camera 5 cm along the shade axis, base yaw fixed at zero. Adjust these to match the actual camera. A turned face can make the estimated distance too large.
